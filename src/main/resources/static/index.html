Privacy Policy
Your Privacy Rights
We respect your data privacy and ensure industry-standard encryption for all transactions.
1. Information We Collect

When registering or reserving a slot on TurfMate, we collect your username, email address, and booking details. Passwords are stored securely in database format.

2. Data Usage & Security

Your email is strictly used for 6-digit OTP verification codes and booking confirmations. We never sell or share user data with third-party advertisers.

3. Payment Privacy

All payment transactions (UPI, QR Code, Card) are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections. Financial credentials like UPI PINs are never stored on our servers.

4. Managing Your Data

You can inspect your complete booking history anytime under "My Bookings" or contact mathanadmin@gmail.com for data removal requests.

Terms of Service
Venue Rules & Conduct
All players must adhere to venue guidelines and footwear requirements during their booked slots.
1. Slot Reservation & Timings

Bookings are valid strictly for the 1-hour slot and date selected. Players are advised to arrive 10 minutes prior to slot start time.

2. Footwear & Turf Maintenance

Players must wear non-marking sports shoes or turf sneakers. Metal studs, cleats, food, and drinks are strictly prohibited on synthetic grass surfaces.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations requested at least 2 hours prior to the slot start time qualify for a full refund or slot rescheduling. Late cancellations are non-refundable.

4. Account Responsibilities

TurfMate reserves the right to suspend accounts engaged in fraudulent activity, double-booking exploitation, or property damage.
