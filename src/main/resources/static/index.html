Your Privacy is Protected

We implement TLS 1.3 encryption and zero third-party data selling policies to ensure player details remain 100% confidential.

256-Bit SSL Secured
No Financial Data Stored
GDPR & DPDP Aligned

1. Information We Collect

When registering an account or reserving a sports turf, we collect your display name, verified Gmail address, and slot reservation timestamps. All passwords are encrypted with adaptive BCrypt hashing.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your email address is strictly used for one-time password (OTP) verification, automated booking confirmation vouchers, and essential status updates. We do not transmit spam or sell your personal data.

3. Payment Privacy & Security

All digital transactions (UPI / QR / Cards) are processed over end-to-end encrypted TLS tunnels. TurfMate never records, handles, or retains UPI PINs, CVVs, or bank login credentials on its servers.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete autonomy over your profile data. You can inspect your past slot records under "My Bookings" or request account removal anytime by writing to mathanadmin@gmail.com.

Ground Rules & Conduct

All players and tournament teams are requested to follow turf surface guidelines and punctual reporting for fair play.

Non-Marking Footwear Only
Arrive 10 Mins Prior
2-Hour Rescheduling Window

1. Slot Reservation & Punctuality

Bookings are locked for the exact 1-hour slot selected. Players must report at least 10 minutes prior to slot kickoff. Late arrivals cannot be compensated with slot extensions due to following reservations.

2. Footwear & Synthetic Grass Maintenance

Only non-marking flat sports sneakers or turf studs are permitted. Metal cleats, spikes, smoking, chewing gum, and sugary beverages are strictly prohibited on the turf turf playing surface.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Slot cancellations or reschedule requests placed at least 2 hours before the booked hour are eligible for a 100% full refund or date-switch voucher. Last-minute cancellations are non-refundable.

4. Fair Play & Conduct Policy

Turf management reserves the unconditional right to suspend accounts or refuse ground entry to patrons engaging in property damage, abusive conduct, or fraudulent double-booking exploits.
